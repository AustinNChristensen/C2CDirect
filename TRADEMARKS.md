# C2CDirect trademark guidelines

The AGPL license applies to software copyright. It does not grant rights to the C2CDirect name,
logos or visual marks except as necessary for reasonable attribution and
truthful description.

## Uses that do not require permission

- Truthfully saying that software is based on, compatible with, or a fork of C2CDirect.
- Linking to the official project or discussing it in articles, presentations, and reviews.
- Retaining copyright, attribution, and license notices required by the AGPL.
- Using the plain-text project name to identify unmodified official software.

## Uses that require written permission

- Naming a hosted service, company, product, or domain in a way that implies it is the official
  C2CDirect service or is endorsed by the project.
- Using C2CDirect logos or confusingly similar visual branding for a commercial service.
- Claiming certification, partnership, sponsorship, or official-provider status.
- Registering company names, domains, social accounts, or application-store listings that are likely
  to cause confusion with the official project.

Forks should use their own name and branding and may state, accurately, that they are derived from
C2CDirect. These interim guidelines may be replaced by a more formal policy without altering AGPL
rights already granted.

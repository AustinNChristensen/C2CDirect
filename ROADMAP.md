# Roadmap

## v1 launch gates

- Final license and trademark policy approved
- External identity provider and Stripe products configured
- Managed PostgreSQL, API runtime, and OTLP collector deployed
- Email/RSS/manual ingestion connected and evaluated on at least 100 consented postings
- Tenant-isolation, billing, backup/restore, deletion/export, and prompt-injection tests complete
- Privacy policy, terms, support path, and incident-response contacts published

## Later

- Recruiter and prime-vendor reputation signals based on user-submitted evidence
- Team/agency workspaces
- Bring-your-own-model and self-hosted embedding options
- Connector SDK and community source adapters
- Benchmarks for C2C detection, rate extraction, ranking quality, cost, and latency
- Optional MLflow experiment tracking if customer deployments require traditional MLOps integration
- Pluggable rerankers and provider-backed embeddings after a consented retrieval benchmark exists

# Product principles

Status: **Accepted**
Last updated: 2026-08-29

## Purpose

C2CDirect helps independent engineers and consultancies find high-value corp-to-corp opportunities
with less noise, better evidence, and clear human control. It optimizes for commercially actionable
opportunities, not the largest possible job count.

## User promises

1. **Quality before volume.** A smaller set of traceable, current, well-paid opportunities is more
   valuable than a large feed of reposts.
2. **Closest available source.** Signal SHOULD resolve each opportunity toward the end client or the
   party controlling the requisition.
3. **Evidence over assertion.** C2C status, rate, client identity, and vendor depth MUST carry evidence
   and confidence rather than being presented as facts when merely inferred.
4. **Tenant ownership.** Customer records, preferences, messages, and decisions MUST remain isolated
   by tenant at both the application and database layers.
5. **Human commercial control.** Signal MAY research, classify, rank, and draft. It MUST NOT submit an
   application, send outreach, accept terms, or make another external commitment without explicit
   authorization for that action.
6. **Terms-aware intake.** Sources MUST use documented APIs, public feeds, licensed data, manual input,
   or user-authorized inbox access. Signal MUST NOT bypass access controls or automate prohibited
   logged-in browsing.
7. **Transparent economics.** Rate quality MUST account for confidence, engagement type, and estimated
   vendor layers; a high headline rate alone is insufficient.
8. **Portable core, valuable service.** The community product MUST remain meaningfully self-hostable.
   Hosted operational intelligence and managed agent access MAY be paid capabilities.

## Success measures

Primary measures SHOULD include direct-or-prime opportunity rate, provenance completeness, confirmed
C2C rate, qualified-opportunity precision, stale/duplicate suppression, submission-to-interview
conversion, and confirmed rate versus advertised rate. Raw ingestion volume is diagnostic, not a
primary success metric.
